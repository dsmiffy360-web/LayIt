Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Who we are

LayIt ("LayIt", "we", "us") is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We're based in the UK and this policy is written to meet UK GDPR. If you have questions, email admin@layit.app.

2. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, and your name/photo if you sign in with GoogleTo create and secure your account
Business profile — business name, contact details, bank details, logo, currencyTo populate the invoices you generate
Job data — room measurements, material choices, scheduling, pricingTo run the cut-list and estimate calculations, and to save your work
Client details you enter — name, address, contact infoSo you can generate invoices and reuse a client across jobs (see note below)
Photos you attach to a jobRoom shots / material receipts you choose to keep with a job record
Billing status (plan, subscription status) — not your card detailsTo know which features your account can access
Push notification device token, if you turn reminders onTo deliver a same-day job reminder to that device
Technical error reports — stack trace, browser/OS, the page you were onTo find and fix bugs

About client details: when you save a client's name and address to use LayIt, you're entering another person's personal data. In that relationship, you are the data controller for your client's information (you decide why you're collecting it and what you do with it), and we act as your processor — we store it and process it only to provide the Service to you. Make sure you have a lawful basis for keeping that information yourself.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service — save your jobs, run calculations, generate invoices and PDFs;
  • To process payments (via Stripe) and manage your subscription;
  • To send a job reminder if you've opted into push notifications;
  • To find and fix errors, using automated crash reports;
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't run behavioural analytics or ad-tracking scripts on the app, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

4. Legal basis

We process most of your data because it's necessary to perform our contract with you (the Terms of Service) — running the Service you signed up for. Error monitoring is a legitimate interest (keeping the Service working). Push notifications are based on your explicit opt-in consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning reminders off.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers ("subprocessors") to run LayIt. None of them can use your data for their own purposes.

ProviderRole

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside the UK/EEA (for example, in the US). Where that happens, they do so under recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from the app itself ("Delete account" at the bottom of your job list) — this immediately and irreversibly deletes your jobs, photos, business profile, and cancels any active subscription.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct it if it's inaccurate (most of this you can edit directly in the app);
  • Erase it — see account deletion above, or contact us;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we've got something wrong.

To exercise any of these, email admin@layit.app.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Row Level Security at the database level, meaning your account can only ever access your own rows — not other users' data. All traffic to the app is encrypted (HTTPS). Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS certified.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits — this is functional, not tracking, and there are no third-party advertising or analytics cookies on the app.

10. Children

LayIt is a professional tool for contractors and isn't directed at, or intended for, children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email admin@layit.app. See also our Terms of Service.
